# Financial Health, Protection & The Arth Protocol Reference

This guide details the **Money Health Score (MHS)** diagnostic framework, the **Arth Protocol**, and the **Protection-First Architecture** borrowed from **ArthMitra** to evaluate client readiness before initiating aggressive F.I.R.E. investing.

---

## 1. The Protection-First Rule (The Core Invariant)

> **"Never advise a client to start an aggressive equity SIP without first establishing their Emergency Shield and Pure Term Insurance."**

Investing in high-volatility equities without a protective moat forces clients to liquidate equity funds during medical emergencies or job loss at distressed market bottoms, permanently wrecking compound growth.

### The 3 Moats of Protection
1. **The Emergency Fund**:
   * Minimum: **6 months of essential living expenses + monthly loan EMIs**.
   * Held strictly in liquid instruments: High-yield savings accounts, Liquid Mutual Funds, or Arbitrage Funds (zero equity market risk).
2. **Pure Term Life Insurance**:
   * Sizing: **15x to 20x annual gross income** + outstanding debts (e.g., home loan balance).
   * Pure term only. Never mix insurance with investment (strictly avoid ULIPs and traditional endowment/money-back policies with their opaque 4%–5% drag).
   * Policy tenure: Until target retirement age (age 55–60), not whole life.
3. **Comprehensive Health Insurance**:
   * Base Health Policy: ₹5L – ₹10L per adult.
   * **Super Top-Up Cover**: ₹25L – ₹50L with a ₹5L/₹10L deductible (exceptional cost-to-coverage ratio).
   * Separate standalone health cover for dependent senior parents (never rely solely on corporate employer health insurance).

## 2. Money Health Score (MHS) Architecture

The Money Health Score is a weighted composite score (0 to 100) across 6 fundamental financial dimensions:

| Dimension | Formula & Target Benchmark | Weight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Emergency Shield** | $\min(100, (\text{Liquid Reserves} / (\text{Monthly Spend} + \text{EMI})) \times \frac{100}{6})$<br>Target: 6 months coverage. | **20%** |
| **2. Protection / Insurance** | Life cover score (ratio of term cover to 15x income) + Health cover adequacy.<br>Target: $\ge 15\text{x income}$. | **25%** |
| **3. Debt Health (FOIR)** | Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio: $\text{FOIR} = \frac{\text{Total Monthly EMIs}}{\text{Monthly Gross Income}}$.<br>Target: $\le 30\%$ (Score 100); $30\%–50\%$ (Score 70); $>50\%$ (Score 30); unpaid credit card balance drops score to 0. | **15%** |
| **4. Investment Allocation** | Deviation from the **"100 - Age" Rule** (e.g. at age 30, equity target $\approx 70\%$, debt $\approx 30\%$). Penalizes extreme under-investing or speculative over-exposure. | **20%** |
| **5. Tax Optimization** | Utilization of eligible tax exemptions (80C, 80CCD, 80D, HRA) and optimal regime selection (Old vs New). | **10%** |
| **6. Retirement Readiness** | Current net worth vs. age-adjusted target on the path to 25x–30x annual expenses. | **10%** |

### MHS Rating Bands
* **Good (85 – 100)**: Resilient financial foundation. Ready for aggressive equity compounding and F.I.R.E. acceleration.
* **Fair (70 – 84)**: Sound baseline; minor vulnerabilities in emergency liquidity or insurance adequacy.
* **Poor (50 – 69)**: Debt drag or lack of protection moat. High vulnerability to income interruption.
* **Critical (< 50)**: Financial distress. Immediate intervention required: freeze discretionary spend, eliminate high-interest debt, fund emergency cash.

## 3. The Arth Protocol (7-Stage Conversational Diagnostic)

When evaluating a client's position, the advisor traverses the 7 levels of the Arth Protocol:

```text
Level 1: Profile     --> Income, essential expenses, discretionary spend, current monthly savings
Level 2: Debt        --> Outstanding loans, interest rates, tenure, reducing vs flat rate, credit cards
Level 3: Protection  --> Health cover, term cover (15x multiple), dependents, nominee registrations
Level 4: Wealth      --> Mutual funds, EPF/PPF, NPS, stocks, gold, real estate, cash
Level 5: Tax         --> Current regime (Old vs New), deductions claimed, HRA, home loan deductions
Level 6: Goals       --> Target retirement age, child education, house purchase, target FIRE corpus
Level 7: Discipline  --> Savings habit consistency, SIP-to-income ratio (target: >= 25% of net income)
```
