## 1. Tactical Sector Playbooks (India - NSE Sectoral Indices)

### Playbook 1: The Banking & Financials Rotation (Early-Cycle Acceleration)
* **Trigger Conditions (3–5 Days Ahead)**:
  * 10-year G-Sec yield stabilizes or drops following dovish RBI commentary.
  * FII Index Futures Long/Short ratio bottoms below 20% and begins ticking upward.
  * Nifty Bank / Nifty 50 relative strength ratio crosses above its 20-day moving average.
* **Execution**:
  * Rotate **+5% to +10%** from Defensives (FMCG) into Large Private Banks (HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank) or Nifty Bank ETF.
* **Invalidation / Stop-Loss**:
  * Invalidation trigger if Gross NPA provisions spike or RBI unexpectedly raises reverse repo / CRR.

## 2. Tax-Smart Rotation Execution Architecture

Tactical rotation creates friction if executed carelessly without considering capital gains taxes:

### In the United Kingdom: The ISA Advantage
* **The Zero-Tax Arbitrage**:
  * Any sector rotation, ETF swap, or individual equity rebalancing executed **inside a Stocks & Shares ISA** incurs **0% Capital Gains Tax and 0% Income Tax**.
  * *Advisory Contract*: All active, tactical sector rotations should be concentrated within the client's **£20,000/year ISA wrapper**, leaving taxable GIAs for passive, buy-and-hold low-turnover global index funds.

### In India: Direct ETF Swaps vs. Mutual Fund Wrappers
* **Tax Cost of Direct Trading**:
  * Selling equity mutual funds or direct stocks held $< 12 \text{ months}$ triggers **20.0% STCG**.
  * Selling equity held $> 12 \text{ months}$ triggers **12.5% LTCG** (above ₹1.25L).
* **The Smart Rotation Vehicle**:
  * For clients who want active sector rotation without incurring personal capital gains tax liabilities:
    * **Dynamic Asset Allocation / Balanced Advantage Funds (BAFs)**: Rotate equity vs. arbitrage/debt dynamically based on market P/E and yield gaps. Internal portfolio turnover within the mutual fund is **completely exempt from capital gains tax**!
    * **Multi-Asset Allocation Funds**: Rotate between Indian Equities, Global Tech, Gold, and Debt without triggering tax until unit redemption.
