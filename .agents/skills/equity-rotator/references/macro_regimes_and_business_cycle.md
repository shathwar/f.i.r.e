# Macroeconomic Regimes & Sector Rotation Playbook

This reference establishes the institutional framework for identifying macroeconomic business cycles and executing systematic sector rotations ahead of broad consensus inflection points.

---

## 1. The 4-Quadrant Macroeconomic Regime Engine

Portfolio rotation is grounded in the interaction between **Growth (accelerating vs. decelerating)** and **Inflation / Policy Rates (rising vs. falling)**:

```text
                  INFLATION / RATES FALLING
                             ▲
                             │
     QUADRANT 4: RECESSION   │   QUADRANT 1: EARLY CYCLE
     • Growth: Decelerating  │   • Growth: Accelerating
     • Policy: Rate Cuts     │   • Policy: Accommodative / Easing
     • Sector Overweight:    │   • Sector Overweight:
       - Pharma & Healthcare │     - Banking & Financial Services
       - FMCG (Staples)      │     - Consumer Discretionary & Auto
       - Quality Low-Debt    │     - Technology & Small-Caps
                             │
 ◄───────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────► GROWTH
                             │
     QUADRANT 3: LATE CYCLE  │   QUADRANT 2: MID CYCLE
     • Growth: Peaking/Slow  │   • Growth: Robust / Expansionary
     • Policy: Tight / Peaks │   • Policy: Neutral to Tightening
     • Sector Overweight:    │   • Sector Overweight:
       - Utilities & Power   │     - Capital Goods & Industrials
       - FMCG & Defensives   │     - Infrastructure & Real Estate
       - Energy & Commodities│     - Manufacturing & Metals
                             │
                             ▼
                  INFLATION / RATES RISING
```

## 4. Predicting Inflections 3 to 10 Days in Advance

Traditional macroeconomic data (GDP prints, quarterly corporate results) are **lagging indicators**. Tactical rotation front-runs consensus using **high-frequency market-implied signals**:

1. **Yield Curve Dynamics (10Y minus 2Y Spread)**:
   - *Curve Steepening from inversion*: Classic signal of transition into **Early Cycle** (rotate from defensives into financials and cyclicals).
   - *Curve Flattening at highs*: Classic signal of transition into **Late Cycle** (rotate into cash buffers, healthcare, and staples).
2. **Relative Strength Breakouts (Ratio Charts)**:
   - When the ratio of **Cyclicals to Defensives** ($\frac{\text{Nifty Auto + Nifty Bank}}{\text{Nifty FMCG + Nifty Pharma}}$) breaks above its 50-day moving average on rising volume, initiate cyclical rotation 3–5 days before media commentary catches on.
3. **Credit Default Spreads & Financial Conditions**:
   - Widening corporate bond spreads precede equity index selloffs by 5–10 trading sessions, giving an early window to rotate into high-quality balance sheets.
